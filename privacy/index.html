Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 • Applies to weaverit.studio and sinteza.weaverit.studio

Weaver IT (Zagreb, Croatia) operates the website weaverit.studio and the online browser game Sinteza at sinteza.weaverit.studio. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, and your rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Information We Collect

  • Gameplay & Account Data: Guest or registered player identifiers, display names, character progression, inventory, decks, Sanctuary upgrades, ELO ratings, and match history required to operate the game.
  • Technical & Security Logs: IP address, browser user-agent, WebSocket session timestamps, and error diagnostics used to prevent abuse, enforce fair play, and keep servers reliable.
  • Local Browser Storage: Preferences such as audio volume, graphics settings, and session tokens stored locally in your browser.

2. Advertising & Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense and Google H5 Games Ads to display optional rewarded advertisements and keep Sinteza free to play. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies or device identifiers to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info.
  • For more details on how Google processes data when you use our site, please review How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected data strictly to operate and improve our websites and multiplayer servers, save your in-game progress, prevent cheating or fraud, comply with legal obligations, and deliver optional rewarded ads. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Data Retention & Your GDPR Rights

As an EU/EEA user (including residents of Croatia), you have the right to access, rectify, export, or request deletion of your personal data, as well as the right to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights or request account deletion, email contact@weaverit.studio.

5. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum digital consent age in your country). If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, please contact contact@weaverit.studio and we will promptly delete it.
