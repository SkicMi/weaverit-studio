About Weaver IT

Independent Web & Multiplayer Game Studio

Weaver IT is an independent game development studio based in Zagreb, Croatia. We specialize in building fast, accessible 3D multiplayer games that run natively in modern web browsers without requiring players to install launchers or large binaries.

What We Build

Our flagship project is Sinteza: Elemental Arena & Extraction MMO — a real-time 3D browser world featuring elemental combat, creature scanning, deckbuilding, Personal Sanctuary housing, and server-authoritative 1v1 ELO Arena matches.

Engineering Focus

  • Zero-friction access: Click a link and play in seconds on desktop web browsers.
  • Authoritative multiplayer architecture: Fair combat, anti-cheat validation, and server-side escrow for competitive duels.
  • Community-first updates: Rapid iteration driven directly by player feedback and playtesting.

About Sinteza

Sinteza is a free-to-play 3D isometric creature-battler MMO. Players scan wild creatures across nine elemental nations, build a Cyber Deck of ability cards, store loot in a personal Sanctuary house, and duel other players in a 1v1 Clash arena. The world is generated procedurally, so every region has its own terrain, weather and wildlife, and every scanned location can later be used as a teleport destination or an arena.

Learn more in our How to Play guide, browse every creature in the Creature Guide, or read the FAQ.

How the game is built

  • Runs in the browser: 3D rendering with WebGL, no downloads or launchers.
  • Server-authoritative: the server validates movement, combat, loot and the in-game economy, which keeps matches fair.
  • Persistent accounts: sign in with Google or play as a guest; progress, creatures, inventory and housing are saved on the server.
  • Regular updates: new features and fixes are released continuously and driven by player feedback.

Advertising & privacy

Sinteza is free thanks to advertising. We use Google AdSense; how cookies and personalised ads work, and how to opt out, is explained in our Privacy Policy. Ads never replace gameplay content.
