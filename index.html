Controls & Studio Philosophy

Weaver IT builds accessible, server-authoritative 3D multiplayer browser games with TypeScript, Three.js, and Node.js. Sinteza is 100% free to play with automatic daily ELO Tickets and optional rewarded ads — no downloads and no pay-to-win walls.

Action Control Description
Move & Sprint W A S D + Shift Explore the Mega-Dome, Elemental Worlds, Dungeons, and your Personal Sanctuary.
Interact F Enter portals, open storage wardrobes & kitchen fridges, sit on furniture, or rest in bed.
Combat & Abilities Left Click / 1–4 Attack with your equipped weapon and cast elemental abilities from your Cyber Deck.
Second Wind R Activate defensive Health & Shield recovery during intense combat.
Deck, Bag & Scanner K / I / L Open your Cyber Deck, Field Pack & Gear inventory, or Synthesis Scanner.
