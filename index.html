Quick Controls & Studio Philosophy

At Weaver IT (Zagreb, Croatia), we build real-time 3D multiplayer games with TypeScript, Three.js, and authoritative Node.js servers so anyone can play in seconds without downloads. Sinteza is 100% free to play — players receive daily ELO Tickets automatically and can optionally watch short rewarded ads for bonus tickets.

Action Control Description
Move & Sprint W A S D + Shift Explore the Mega-Dome, Elemental Worlds, Dungeons, and your Personal Sanctuary.
Interact F Enter portals, open storage wardrobes & kitchen fridges, sit on furniture, or rest in bed.
Combat & Abilities Left Click / 1–4 Swing or fire your equipped weapon and cast elemental ability cards.
Second Wind R Trigger emergency HP & Shield recovery in combat.
Deck, Bag & Scanner K / I / L Equip gear, inspect extracted loot, and scan wild creatures or terrain locations.
