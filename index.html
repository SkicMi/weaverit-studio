Flagship Title: Sinteza — Elemental Arena & Extraction MMO

Sinteza drops you into a living elemental frontier connected to the Sinteza Mega-Dome hub. Explore open-world biomes and multi-room dungeons, scan wild elemental creatures and arcane locations into collectible cards, customize your Personal Sanctuary, and challenge other explorers in high-stakes 1v1 ELO Arena duels.

Exploration & Extraction

Open-World Biomes & Dungeons

Travel from the Sinteza Dome through elemental portals into procedurally rich zones. Gather resources, open chests, defeat roaming enemies, and extract safely back to the Dome to lock in your field loot.

Scanning & Deckbuilding

Elemental Creatures & Ability Cards

Use your Synthesis Scanner in the wild to capture creature DNA, elemental abilities, and terrain locations as permanent cards. Combine Fire, Water, Nature, Electric, Steel, Flesh, Light, and Dark synergies to build your competitive loadout.

Competitive PvP

1v1 ELO Arena & Card Wagers

Test your build against real players in ranked 1v1 Arena matches. Climb the global ELO leaderboard, use daily ELO Tickets to enter matches, and participate in optional card-wager duels with server-enforced escrow protection.

Player Housing

Personal Sanctuary

Step through the red Sanctuary portal in the Dome to visit your private multi-floor house. Store supplies in kitchen fridges and tiered storage wardrobes, sit at furniture, and rest in bed to restore Health and Stamina between expeditions.

How to Get Started in Sinteza

Jumping into Sinteza takes less than ten seconds. You can start immediately as a Guest explorer or sign in to keep your inventory, ELO rating, and Sanctuary upgrades synced across sessions.

Our Studio Philosophy

At Weaver IT, we believe multiplayer games should be accessible from any computer without multi-gigabyte downloads or invasive launchers. Sinteza is built from the ground up with TypeScript, Three.js, and authoritative Node.js WebSocket servers to deliver responsive 3D gameplay directly in Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari.

We keep Sinteza free to play with fair progression. Players receive daily login ELO Tickets automatically and can optionally watch short rewarded ads to earn bonus tickets without pay-to-win walls.
