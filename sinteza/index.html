Official Player Guide

Sinteza: Elemental Arena & Extraction MMO

Published by Weaver IT • Zagreb, Croatia

Sinteza is a free-to-play 3D browser multiplayer game that blends open-world elemental exploration, extraction loot runs, creature scanning, personal housing, and competitive 1v1 ELO Arena combat.

Play Sinteza in Your Browser →

1. The Sinteza Mega-Dome (Safe Hub)

Every journey begins inside the Sinteza Mega-Dome, a social safe zone where PvP is disabled. From the Dome you can inspect global ELO leaderboards, chat with other explorers, manage your deck and inventory, access vending stations, or step onto portals leading to Elemental Worlds, Dungeons, and your Personal Sanctuary.

2. Elemental Worlds & Extraction Loop

Portals inside the Dome transport you into Elemental World Alpha, Elemental World Beta, and instanced dungeons. In the field, enemies patrol territory and guard valuable loot chests. Any field loot you collect stays in your expedition bag until you extract safely back to the Dome — making positioning, stamina management, and tactical retreats critical.

3. Synthesis Scanner & Card Collection

Your Synthesis Scanner lets you scan wild creatures and strategic terrain points across the world. Successful scans unlock permanent cards for your collection, including Creature cards, Ability cards, and Map Location cards. Mix elemental affinities — Fire, Water, Nature, Electric, Steel, Flesh, Light, and Dark — to counter opponent strategies.

4. Personal Sanctuary

Stand on the red Sanctuary portal in the Dome and press F to enter your private multi-floor house. Inside your Sanctuary you can store food and consumables in your kitchen fridge, organize equipment inside tiered storage wardrobes, sit on sofas and chairs, and lie down in your bed to steadily regenerate Health and Stamina before your next run.

5. Ranked 1v1 ELO Arena & Tickets

Competitive players can queue for 1v1 ELO Arena matches directly from the HUD or challenge another explorer in the Dome. New accounts start with ELO Tickets and automatically receive daily login tickets each UTC day. Players can also optionally watch a short rewarded video ad (up to twice daily) to earn bonus ELO Tickets.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Is Sinteza free to play? Yes. Sinteza is 100% free to play in your browser with no downloads.
  • Which browsers are supported? Modern desktop versions of Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari with WebGL enabled.
  • Do I need an account to try the game? No — you can jump in immediately as a Guest and upgrade to a saved account whenever you like.
